THE COMPANY

A considered approach to textile manufacturing.

Maison Fabrics was created around a simple idea: clients should be able to commission fabric around their vision, rather than compromise their vision around whatever is already available.

Our work brings together material selection, construction, colour and finishing into one conversation. The result is a fabric designed for its intended use and aesthetic.
